QUY TAC HUONG NHIN VAO LUOI (ap dung cho ca doi)
- Doi o nua san DUOI: mat huong len luoi -> dung ho BACK (BACK, BACK-LEFT,
  BACK-RIGHT tuy huong di chuyen). Day la goc mac dinh cua doi nguoi choi.
- Doi o nua san TREN: mat huong xuong luoi -> dung ho FRONT
  (FRONT, FRONT-LEFT, FRONT-RIGHT).
- Khi cau thu di chuyen ngang / cheo: chon goc SIDE hoac 3/4 tuong ung
  theo vector di chuyen, nhung than van uu tien huong ve luoi khi dung yen
  va khi phong thu.
- Map animation: vector huong (dx, dy tren san isometric) -> 1 trong 8 goc
  cua sheet turnaround, lay goc gan nhat.
