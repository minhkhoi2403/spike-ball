KHANH HAI WAVES

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE standalone original volleyball uniform concept sheet for fictional team KHANH HAI WAVES. Reference image is the USER-APPROVED sprite style; preserve its chibi proportions, small thin arms, large heads, small compact bodies, white shoes, short legs, bold dark pixel outlines, sparse true pixel-art shading. Do not enlarge muscles or arms. This is a character-only design sheet, no court, no ball, no background props, no game UI. Plain light warm-gray background. Landscape composition with exactly EIGHT full-body sprites in two rows of four, equal spacing. Row 1 HOME, row 2 AWAY. Within each row: columns 1 and 2 show the SAME regular player number 7 FRONT then BACK; columns 3 and 4 show the SAME libero number 1 FRONT then BACK. All regular player views use the black curly haired player from the reference; all libero views use the orange spiky haired player from the reference. Faces/hair/skin/proportions match across front/back and home/away. Both regular and libero wear SLEEVELESS jerseys like reference. HOME regular uniform: deep navy with golden yellow collar and thin side piping, ivory white number, navy shorts. AWAY regular uniform: ivory white with navy shoulder panels and gold side piping, navy number, navy shorts. BOTH home and away LIBERO uniform: brilliant canary yellow jersey with navy trim, dark navy number, navy shorts. Uniform construction identity: a tiny stepped wave stripe confined to the lower side panels. Large simple readable block pixel jersey numbers centered on chest and on back, consistent numbers 7 for regular player and 1 for libero, matching front/back. NO PLAYER NAMES, no text on clothes except digits, no sponsors, no logos, no watermarks, no signature, no real team branding. Above whole sheet print team name exactly 'KHANH HAI WAVES' in clean restrained dark block typography; label first row 'HOME', second 'AWAY' outside sprite area. Column headings exactly 'FRONT', 'BACK', 'LIBERO FRONT', 'LIBERO BACK'. All text outside uniforms. Full sprites entirely visible, generous whitespace, no decorative borders, cohesive professional pixel art costume proof. Match the provided reference's cute pixel character design, NOT realistic humans. Flat pixel colors, no soft gradients, no glossy lighting. Differentiate regular and libero shirts overwhelmingly by brightness and hue.
